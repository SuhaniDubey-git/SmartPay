SmartPay is an independently developed project inspired by the concept of group expense-splitting applications, including the original SplitApp project.

The idea was to build upon this concept with a fresh identity, redesigned user interface, and additional improvements to create a simpler and more intuitive expense management experience.

SmartPay is my implementation and redesign, developed with inspiration from existing expense-sharing platforms.
